Hilo — Support

Purchases

Your first episode is free. To restore a purchase on a new device, open the paywall of a locked episode and tap Restore purchases. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress

Your progress and saved words are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Voices

For more natural speech, download Enhanced or Premium voices in iOS Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.

Contact

Open an issue at github.com/BavetT1/Hiloprivacy/issues.
