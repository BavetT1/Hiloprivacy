Hilo — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Hilo is an interactive story for language learners. The app has no accounts and no servers of its own.

What we collect

Nothing. Your progress, chosen language and level, collected clues and study cards are stored only on your device. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Translation and speech

Word translation uses Apple's on-device Translation framework, and voices use Apple's built-in speech synthesis. Text you tap is processed on your device by iOS.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Children

Hilo does not knowingly collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: see the support page.

Кратко по-русски: приложение не собирает данные; прогресс хранится только на вашем устройстве; покупки обрабатывает Apple.

En resumen: la app no recoge datos; tu progreso se guarda solo en tu dispositivo; las compras las gestiona Apple.
